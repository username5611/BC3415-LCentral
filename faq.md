# Frequently asked questions

## How big are the classes?
At most 8 students per class.

## Can parents sit in on a lesson?
Parents may observe the first 15 minutes of the trial lesson. Regular lessons are not open to parents so students can focus.

## What should my child bring?
Stationery, a calculator for Sec 1 and above, and their school textbook if they have questions from school.

## Do you give homework?
Yes, weekly homework that takes about 45 minutes. It is marked and returned at the next lesson.

## Is there online tuition?
No, all lessons are in person at the centre.

## What happens if it rains heavily or there is a haze alert?
Lessons continue as scheduled unless the centre announces a cancellation by WhatsApp at least 2 hours before.

## How do I track my child's progress?
Parents receive a WhatsApp progress update after every topical test, every 4 weeks.

## How do I enrol?
Book a trial lesson first. If your child enjoys it, tell the front desk to enrol them into the class. Enrolment needs the registration fee and deposit.
