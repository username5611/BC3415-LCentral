# Centre policies

## Make-up lessons
Students may make up a missed lesson in another class of the same subject and level, subject to seat availability. Up to 2 make-up lessons are allowed per term. The front desk must be told at least 24 hours before the missed lesson. With a medical certificate, notice can be given on the day and the lesson does not count towards the limit. Make-up lessons must be taken within the same month as the missed lesson and are not carried forward. Missed lessons without notice are not refunded or made up.

## Rescheduling
A one-off change of lesson date is treated as a make-up lesson. A permanent change of class timing follows the changing classes rule: effective from the start of a month, with 1 week's notice.

## Withdrawal and refunds
To withdraw, give 4 weeks' written notice by email or the front desk. The deposit is refunded within 14 days after the last lesson if notice was given and fees are fully paid. Monthly fees already paid are not refundable. Refund requests outside this policy are reviewed by the centre manager.

## Lesson cancellations by the centre
If the centre cancels a lesson, for example because a tutor is ill, a replacement lesson is arranged or the lesson is credited to the next month's fees.

## Conduct and safety
Students must be respectful to tutors and classmates. Parents with concerns about a tutor, a classmate or their child's wellbeing should raise it with the centre manager, who will respond within 1 working day. Students must be picked up within 15 minutes after the lesson ends.
