# Programmes and levels

## Primary (P3 to P6)
Subjects: Maths, Science and English.
P6 classes focus on PSLE preparation from Term 2, including timed practice papers.

## Lower Secondary (Sec 1 to Sec 2)
Subjects: Maths, Science and English.

## Upper Secondary (Sec 3 to Sec 4)
Subjects: E Maths, A Maths, Physics, Chemistry and English.
Sec 4 classes focus on O-Level preparation from Term 2.

## Placement
New students may be asked to do a short 20-minute diagnostic quiz before their trial lesson so the tutor can gauge their level. There is no entrance test and no student is turned away because of their grades.

## Changing classes
Students can switch to another class of the same subject and level at the start of any month, subject to seat availability. Tell the front desk at least 1 week before the new month.
