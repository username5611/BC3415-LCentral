# About BrightPath Learning Centre

## Overview
BrightPath Learning Centre is a small-group tuition centre in Singapore for Primary 3 to Secondary 4 students. Classes have at most 8 students so tutors can give individual attention.

The front desk is staffed Tuesday to Friday 2pm-9pm and Saturday to Sunday 9am-6pm. The centre is closed on Mondays and public holidays.

## Lesson format
Each lesson is 2 hours, once a week. Students receive worksheets, weekly homework and a topical test every 4 weeks. Parents get a short progress update by WhatsApp after every topical test.

## Terms
The year has 4 terms that follow the MOE school calendar. There are no lessons during the June holidays week 1 and the December holidays weeks 3-4.
