# Fees

## Monthly tuition fees (per subject, 4 lessons a month)
- Primary 3 and Primary 4: $220 per month
- Primary 5 and Primary 6: $260 per month
- Secondary 1 and Secondary 2: $280 per month
- Secondary 3 and Secondary 4: $320 per month
Months with 5 lessons are charged at the same monthly fee.

## Registration fee and deposit
A one-time registration fee of $50 is payable on enrolment. A refundable deposit equal to one month of fees is collected per subject.

## Trial lessons
A trial lesson costs $30. The $30 is deducted from the first month's fees if the student enrols within 7 days of the trial. Each student can have one trial per subject.

## Discounts
Siblings enrolled at the same time get 5% off each sibling's monthly fees. Students taking 3 or more subjects get 10% off the total monthly fees. Discounts cannot be combined; the higher discount applies.

## Payment
Fees are due by the 7th of each month by PayNow, bank transfer or card at the front desk. A late fee of $20 applies to payments received after the 14th.
